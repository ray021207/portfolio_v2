### Project 1 — Adidas: Midsole Comfort Perception Study
**Industry-sponsored eye-tracking research | August–September 2026**

Owned the analysis workflow for an ASU eye-tracking study investigating how visual attention to shoe-midsole designs relates to perceived basketball comfort. Working with Professor Aurel Coza, built a reproducible Python pipeline from raw 120 Hz Tobii exports through preprocessing, gaze calibration correction, image-level segmentation, statistical analysis, visualization, and stakeholder-ready reporting.

- Processed gaze and fixation data across 11 midsole designs, organized into front/middle/heel regions and linked to participants’ like/dislike judgments.
- Developed automated midsole region detection, gaze heatmaps and 13 eye-tracking metrics, with quality-control checks for calibration drift, timestamp units, tracking loss, and misleading correlations.
- Found an exploratory association between attention to the middle region and design preference (**R² = 0.301; Spearman ρ = 0.61; p = .048** at the design level). **Small sample: four main-study participants across six sessions; do not portray this as a validated general finding.**
- Prepared research reports, charts and a concise stakeholder synopsis for the Adidas team.

**Methods:** Python, pandas, Tobii eye-tracking data, computer vision, data validation, regression, Spearman correlation, visual analytics.

### Project 2 — Mindsigns: Head-Impact and Brain-Activity Study
**Ongoing research**

Supporting an ASU research study investigating how brain activity and physiological signals change following head impacts in athletes. The research involves EEG recordings, wearable and head-impact sensors, balance and heart-rate variability (HRV) assessments, and app-based symptom and sleep monitoring. My work supports the study's participant-testing, data-collection, and data-organization workflow.
