Wearable sensing / Systems concept

A wearable that asks what kind of tired.

An early-warning concept separating physical load and psychological burnout rather than treating recovery as a single number.

The question

Fatigue can arise from training load, stress and recovery patterns, but a single fitness score collapses those mechanisms. The project considered how physiological sensing could provide separate clues about physical and psychological strain.

Signal approach

Photoplethysmography and heart-rate variability were explored alongside electrodermal activity. EDA can be separated into slowly varying tonic activity and transient phasic responses, supporting different interpretations of arousal and load.

A systems-design problem

The proposed device combines multiple time-dependent sensor streams with baseline tracking, careful signal quality checks and a human-readable alert layer. Sensor fusion is useful only if the device can distinguish missing or noisy measurements from genuine change.

What has not been demonstrated

The earlier portfolio documents a wearable concept, not a completed clinical intervention or prospective burnout study. Before claiming useful early warnings, a prototype would need ground-truth data, baseline calibration and validated outcomes.

Evidence & limits

Concept/design work based on prior portfolio notes. No clinically validated burnout prediction is claimed.
