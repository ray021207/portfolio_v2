CAMERA-TRAP GENERALIZATION · RESEARCH PAPER

Do camera-trap species classifiers survive a new camera?

A CCT20 study where 86.0% random-image accuracy fell to 50.9% on unseen cameras. MegaDetector crops improved unseen-camera accuracy to 70.4%, while revealing the limits of location generalization.
