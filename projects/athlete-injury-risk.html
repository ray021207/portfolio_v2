The missing label

MHEALTH includes accelerometer, gyroscope and ECG-style sensor time series annotated with physical activities, not actual future injuries. The project defined heuristic risk labels to create a supervised learning benchmark.

Comparing model families

The team evaluated classical ML pipelines alongside temporal deep-learning architectures including LSTM and a 1D CNN. The CNN learned directly from time-series windows while classical models used engineered statistics.

The suspiciously perfect model

An early Random Forest returned 100% accuracy. Instead of reporting it, we examined imbalance and validation controls, then revised the evaluation around subject-wise separation. The new scores were lower, and more defensible.

What the score does and doesn't mean

The reported 99.79% F1 reflects prediction of deterministic labels generated from those same sensor channels. It does not establish real injury prediction. Actual longitudinal injury outcomes are needed before such a claim could be tested.

Interpretation & limits

Experimental proxy-label research. The scores do not validate clinical or real-world injury forecasting.
