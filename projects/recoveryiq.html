The workflow problem

Rehabilitation includes small changes that can be hard to communicate between appointments. The project explored a shared system for patient self-assessment, practitioner review, movement recordings and progress tracking.

What the prototype includes

Patients can complete guided intake and timed movement scans, record symptoms and review progress. A MediaPipe PoseLandmarker pipeline supports video-based range-of-motion and symmetry calculations. Practitioners can review assessment reports, progress and care-plan summaries.

What makes it more than a pose demo

The prototype brings movement measurement into a dual-role product: intake, adherence and session history surround the model output. This is important because a precise joint angle is not meaningful if the user cannot act on it or if the camera setup is unreliable.

The evidence gap

Camera viewpoint, joint occlusion, lighting and person-to-person variation can affect pose estimates. The repository documents implementation features but not a clinical outcomes trial or accuracy validation against motion-capture instrumentation.

Interpretation & limits

Hackathon prototype, not a validated medical device or a replacement for professional rehabilitation advice.
