The reporting problem

Climate claims often arrive as one number without context. For journalists and readers, location, time range, measurement method and uncertainty are just as important as the trend line itself.

The dashboard experience

Glacious offers question and claim-validation modes alongside satellite-style maps, region comparisons and trend visualizations. Its documented scope includes glacier-area trends, snow cover, permafrost conditions and biodiversity indicators.

The architecture

The Streamlit interface coordinates natural-language query parsing, report modes, region data loaders and chart modules. Maps built with Folium and Plotly-style interactive charts let a user explore multiple indicators rather than a single headline.

The crucial data qualification

The public repository explicitly says the demonstration data are synthetic, informed by published literature patterns, and that production use would require real satellite feeds and validation against authoritative datasets. Visual plausibility is not evidence of live measurements.

Evidence & limits

Hackathon demonstration. The data displayed in the documented demo are synthetic; this is not a live, independently verified satellite-science service.
