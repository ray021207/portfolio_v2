03 / About

I'd rather
be curious.

M.S. Robotics & Autonomous Systems (AI), Arizona State University
B.S. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, REVA University

I've always been drawn to nature, but my education took me into AI, computer vision, and robotics. Now I'm figuring out how to bring those worlds together.

I enjoy the unglamorous parts of engineering—the dataset that doesn't make sense, the sensor that lies to you, the evaluation that reveals an uncomfortable truth. That's usually where the interesting work begins.

I'm especially interested in perception and field-ready AI: the kinds of systems that leave the controlled demo behind and still have to earn someone's trust.
